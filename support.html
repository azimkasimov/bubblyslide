Bubble Slide – Help

Need help? Email write2kasimov@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, and in the game open Settings to find the version number at the bottom. We usually reply within two working days.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close the game completely and open it again: the store hands over any purchase that didn't finish. For one-time items (starter pack, bundles, heroes, coin doubler, No Ads), open Settings → Restore purchases. If gems are still missing, email us with the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt.

I have a new phone. How do I keep my progress?

Your progress is backed up to your own account automatically: iCloud on iPhone and iPad, Google Play Games on Android. Sign in to the same account on the new phone, install Bubble Slide and open it; your levels, items and gems come back by themselves. Then use Settings → Restore purchases for one-time items.

How do friend races work?

Finish any level and tap Challenge a friend. Send the message to a friend. When they open the link (or copy the whole message and tap Party → Paste a code in the game), they race your ghost on exactly the same slide.

How do two players play on one phone?

Open Party → 2 Players. The player on the left steers with a finger on the left half of the screen, the player on the right on the right half.

Can I turn off ads or reminders?

Reward videos only play when you choose to watch one. Adults may see a short ad between some levels; the No Ads purchase removes them. Players under 18 never see ads between levels. Reminders can be switched off in Settings → Reminders.

For parents

Every purchase and every link out of the game asks a grown-up question first. You can also block purchases in your phone's settings (Screen Time on iPhone, "Require authentication for purchases" in Google Play). There is no chat and no free text in the game.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on the phone. The cloud backup can be deleted in your iCloud or Google Play Games settings. To delete anything else linked to your installation, email us. See the privacy policy for details.
